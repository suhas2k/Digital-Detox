Deconstructing the Cycle

The three invisible behavioral traps.

Every interface element is optimized to eliminate natural stopping cues and maintain continuous engagement.

01
The Infinite Scroll Loop
Physical newspapers have a final page; books have chapters. Modern digital feeds have neither. By removing stopping boundaries, the brain never receives an organic cue to exit.
02
Intermittent Variable Rewards
Identical to a casino slot machine: pulling down to refresh sometimes delivers a high-dopamine notification, sometimes nothing. This unpredictability cements the checking addiction.
03
Engineered Frictionlessness
Autoplay, face-unlock, and 1-tap notifications remove every micro-barrier between an unconscious impulse and an hour lost down an algorithmic rabbit hole.
Quick Self-Reflection

Does this sound familiar in your daily life?

Read these common micro-moments. If you resonate with two or more, a digital reset will yield immediate relief.

  • Reaching for your phone before your feet touch the floor every morning
  • Experiencing "phantom vibrations" in your pocket when no notification arrived
  • Opening social media automatically while waiting at a 30-second red light or elevator
  • Feeling uneasy or restless when eating a meal alone without a podcast or video playing
  • Losing 45 minutes of evening wind-down time to mindless short-form video loops
