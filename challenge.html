The 7-Day Protocol

Reclaim your life, one day at a time.

No dramatic extremes or throwing your device away. A gradual, science-backed progression that introduces one sustainable habit each morning.

What You Reclaim • Mental Clarity & Real World Presence
Day 01
The Notification Audit
Disable all lock-screen alerts except direct human calls and SMS. Reclaim control over when you choose to open an app.
Day 02
The 30-Minute Morning Sanctuary
Do not touch your phone for the first 30 minutes after waking. Let your cortisol spike naturally with sunlight and water instead of emails.
Day 03
Screen-Free Dining
Eat all breakfast, lunch, and dinner meals without a screen in sight. Reconnect with the sensory experience of eating.
Day 04
The Grayscale Switch
Set your phone display to black-and-white. Experience how instantly boring social feeds and colorful app icons become.
Day 05
The 90-Minute Focus Sprint
Put your device in another room or inside a drawer for 90 consecutive minutes of unbroken creative or professional work.
Day 06
The Bedroom Exile
Charge your phone outside the bedroom overnight. Wake up to a physical alarm clock and enjoy a peaceful, phone-free evening wind-down.
Day 07
The Half-Day Analog Immersion
Spend 4 continuous weekend hours completely disconnected. Go for a long nature walk, read a book, journal, or spend unhurried time with family.
Sustainable Living

After the reset: an intentional baseline.

The goal of the 7-day challenge isn't lifelong asceticism—it is proving to yourself that you are in control of your device, not the other way around.
