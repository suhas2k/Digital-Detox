Daily Architecture

A natural daily rhythm without screen overload.

Four key transition zones where establishing physical boundaries makes the biggest cognitive difference.

Morning
The First 60 Minutes
Use a $10 analog alarm clock and leave your phone charging in another room. Wake up, drink water, stretch, or look outside before looking at the world's emergencies on a glass screen.
Work
Out of Reach & Sight
Research shows that even a powered-off phone on your desk consumes cognitive bandwidth. Place your device inside a drawer or across the room during 90-minute focus sprints.
Meals
Sacred Screen-Free Dining
Eat without scrolling videos or answering emails. Taste your food, let your mind wander, or engage in undivided conversation with family or colleagues.
Evening
The 9 PM Digital Sunset
Switch on Do Not Disturb and park your phone on its charging dock away from your bed 60 minutes before sleep. Read a physical paper book or journal to signal rest to your nervous system.
5-Minute Settings Tweaks

Friction changes everything.

Simple software adjustments that immediately neutralize the addictive pull of your device.

01

Turn on Grayscale

Apps use vibrant candy colors to stimulate attention. Converting your display to black-and-white strips away visual dopamine.

02

Audit Badge Counts

Disable all red notification dots. Check apps on your own schedule instead of reacting to urgent visual cues.

03

Clean Your Home Screen

Keep only utility tools (Maps, Notes, Calendar, Camera) on your main screen. Move social media apps into folders on the last page.

04

Delete Infinite Apps

Access social feeds and video platforms exclusively through desktop browsers where algorithms are far easier to close.
